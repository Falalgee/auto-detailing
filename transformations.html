The Calibrated Light Tunnel

Sunlight is naturally forgiving, scattering off environmental reflections that disguise sub-surface swirl marks and rotary gouges. Inside Obsidian's bespoke subterranean inspection tunnel, we isolate paint under 5,000K daylight-balanced luminaire arrays.

01

High CRI Spectrum Arrays

Ra 98+ color rendering exposes true pigment undertones, orange peel waviness, and micro-holograms impossible to detect under standard shop halogens.

02

Coaxial Spot & Linear Grazing Emitters

Raking angle fixtures cast acute shadows over sanding pigtails, resin sink marks, and dry-spray textures across complex curvilinear panels.

03

Zero Ambient Stray Scatter

Matte carbon-black chamber walls extinguish parasitic reflections, guaranteeing that only the vehicle clear coat speaks.
