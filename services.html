The Obsidian Atelier Protocol

Every commission passes through an uncompromising verification lifecycle before client handover. We adhere strictly to ISO/IEC 17025 compliant metrology standards.

01 biotech

Cleanroom Ultrasonic Mapping

Prior to any mechanical contact, the vehicle enters a climate-stabilized ISO Class 7 cleanroom. A 64-point ultrasonic probe scans the thickness of substrate primer, base, and clear coats to map every square centimeter in an immutable digital ledger.

Scan Density 64 Ref Points
02 light_mode

Multi-Spectrum Illumination

Our bays feature overhead and articulated side lamps calibrated through three distinct color temperatures: 3000K warm incandescent for micro-holograms, 4500K daylight neutral, and 6500K surgical cool for subsurface buffer trails and pigment fleck pop.

Inspection Lights CRI 98+ Ra
03 microwave

Short-Wave Infrared Baking

Rather than standard passive evaporation cure, coatings undergo precision thermal irradiation via computer-governed short-wave IR emitters. Heat penetrates beneath the glass shell, curing the molecular bond from within the clear resin outward.

Thermal Curve 65°C Regulated
