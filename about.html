The Atelier Philosophy.

Where meticulous material chemistry meets obsessive surface perfection. We treat bespoke coachbuilt vehicles and rare hypercars as kinetic art pieces deserving of conservator-grade discretion.

Conceived by Falalu Creative Studio.

Obsidian Auto Studio emerged not as a conventional detailer, but as an architectural response to the violent, high-friction methods standard across automotive trade networks.

Our space treats bespoke coachbuilt vehicles, rare historic silhouettes, and limited hypercars as kinetic art pieces deserving of conservator-grade discretion.

Every clear coat contains an irreplaceable factory signature. Our commitment is thermodynamic and microscopic: we do not grind away paint for fleeting gloss. We manipulate refraction indices, level nanoscale clear coat valleys, and fortify molecular chains.

The Three Studio Benchmarks

Standardized parameters engineered to eradicate environmental interference before a single tool contacts the bodywork.

air

Surgical HEPA Cleanroom

Airborne particulate matter is the quiet nemesis of optical clarity. Our bay utilizes positive-pressure micro-filtered air scrubbers that cycle 4,200 m³ per hour, preventing airborne micro-grit from nesting into wet ceramic matrices during the critical 18-hour polymerization window.

wb_twilight

5000K Solar Simulation

Defects that vanish under dim garage spotlights burn glaringly under noon daylight. Our overhead canopy array mimics both sterile inspection conditions and high-noon Mediterranean sunlight, illuminating micro-marring down to 120 nanometer troughs.

pan_tool

Concours Master Guild

Machines do not direct the work; tactile human intuition does. Each vehicle is attended exclusively by master technicians trained on single-stage cellulose enamels, ultra-soft modern Japanese clears, and bespoke pre-preg carbon weaves.

Our Non-Negotiable Tenets

Conventional shops optimize for turnover, churning through bays to maximize volume. We impose deliberate constraints to safeguard paint longevity for generations.

01

Volume Cap: 4 Motorcars Per Week

We refuse overflow. Curing protocols require stagnant thermodynamic conditions; no vehicle is hurried off a rack to free up workshop capacity.

02

Acoustic & Eddy Metrology First

No rotary or dual-action polisher touches a surface before a comprehensive 72-point ultrasonic depth survey confirms healthy clear coat thresholds across every body seam.

03

Immutable Provenance Log

Clients receive an encrypted, signed digital dossier charting baseline micron depths, correction deltas, chemical bond certifications, and temperature telemetry.
